Common questions
What if the invoice is handwritten or a bit messy?+
AI reads it regardless - handwriting, stamps, creases, whatever's actually on the page. If anything's genuinely unclear, your processor can correct it before approving.
Do I need to change how my staff currently work?+
Barely. Dispatch keeps doing exactly what they do now, they just photograph the invoice instead of filing it. Nothing else about receiving changes.
I'm a small business and do everything myself - does this still work?+
Yes. One person can scan, review, and approve their own invoices - there's no requirement to have separate dispatch and processor staff.
What if the AI gets something wrong?+
Nothing is final until your processor reviews and approves it. Anything can be corrected before it becomes part of your real records.
Can I get my data out if I ever want to leave?+
Yes, any time - export your full history to CSV or Excel whenever you like. It's your data.

Our Story

GRV Scanner started with a simple, everyday frustration: hours spent manually typing supplier invoices into spreadsheets, line by line, invoice by invoice - time that should have gone into actually running the business.

We built GRV Scanner to solve that one problem properly. Point your phone at an invoice, and AI reads it for you - the supplier, the items, the prices, the VAT - turning a stack of paper into clean, usable data in seconds.

Today, GRV Scanner helps businesses track suppliers, catch price increases before they add up, and keep their stock and spending under control - without the manual admin that used to eat up every afternoon.

Who do we serve

GRV Scanner is built for businesses in South Africa and its neighbouring countries that receive stock and get supplier invoices with it. If goods arrive and an invoice has to be captured, checked and filed, this is made for you - whether that is one shop or a group of branches.

The kinds of business we work with
Different businesses, the same everyday problem: invoices to capture.

Restaurants and retail

Deliveries arrive every day from many different suppliers, each with their own invoice. GRV Scanner turns every one of those invoices into a clean record the moment the stock lands.

You see what you are really paying, catch a price that crept up since the last delivery, and stop the same invoice being captured twice.

Daily deliveries, many suppliers

Wholesale and supermarkets

High invoice volume is where manual capturing hurts most. Each branch includes 2,000 scans every month, with no limit on how many staff you add.

Every product keeps a full price history, so you can see exactly when a supplier changed a price and what it has done to your costs.

High volume, item-level detail

Multi-branch groups

Every branch is its own company and has its own subscription, so each one is billed separately, exactly as a separate business would be.

A group Admin can still switch between all branches in the group from one login, and sees each branch on its own records.

One login, separate branch billing

One-person businesses

You do not need a separate team. One person can scan the invoice, review what the AI read, and approve it.

Nothing is locked in until you approve it, so you stay in control even when you are doing everything yourself.

Scan, review, approve - all you
Who does what in your team
Three roles, so the right people see and do the right things.

Dispatch

The person who receives the delivery. They photograph the supplier invoice on their phone and it uploads straight away.

They do not need any accounting knowledge - their job is simply to scan.

Processor

Reviews what the AI read, corrects anything that needs it, then approves the invoice so it becomes a clean, locked record.

Admin decides exactly which rights each processor has, such as approving, exporting to your accounting system, editing the item list or managing Dispatch staff.

Admin

The owner or manager. Sets up staff and their rights, manages suppliers and VAT rates, and sees spending and price insights.

In a multi-branch group, the Admin can switch between all branches in the group.

How does it work?

GRV Scanner turns the supplier invoices that arrive with your stock into clean, approved records. Your team photographs the invoice, AI reads it, a person checks it, and the data is ready for your reports and your accounting system.

Scan on a phoneAI reads itYou approve itExport anywhere
From delivery to approved record
Six steps, and only one of them needs a person with any accounting knowledge.
1
Admin

Set up your branch

Sign up, add your supplier list and invite your team. This is done once.

  • Add suppliers and mark each as VAT registered or exempt
  • Invite Dispatch and Processor staff, and choose exactly what each processor may do
  • Add more branches later - each is its own company with its own subscription
2
Dispatch

Scan the invoice when stock arrives

The person receiving the delivery photographs the supplier invoice on their phone. No typing and no paper to file.

  • Works straight from a phone camera
  • Uploads the moment the photo is taken
3
AI

AI reads every line

AI reads the invoice - printed, handwritten, stamped or creased - and turns it into clean data in seconds.

  • Supplier, every item and its price
  • VAT worked out from your own supplier and product VAT settings
4
Automatic

Mistakes and surprises are flagged

Before anything is approved, the system checks the invoice against your history.

  • Duplicate invoice detection, so the same invoice is not captured twice
  • Price increase alerts when a product costs more than last time
5
Processor

Review, correct, approve

A processor checks what the AI read, fixes anything that needs it, and approves. Nothing becomes a real record until then.

  • Edit or reject individual line items
  • Approved invoices are locked in as clean records
6
Admin

See it, export it, use it

Approved data feeds your reports straight away, and goes to your accounting system when you want it.

  • Spend by supplier or department, with trends and charts
  • Daily Summary you can save as a PDF
  • Export to CSV, TSV or Excel, with columns tailored for Sage, Pastel or Xero
